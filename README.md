**Varban Auto Flex** is a digital automotive marketplace designed to make buying and selling vehicles in Kenya simpler, faster, and more transparent.

The platform connects **vehicle buyers, private sellers, car yards, and automotive businesses** in one digital marketplace where users can discover vehicles, search by their preferred specifications, review vehicle information, and connect with sellers.

---

## 🚗 How Varban Auto Flex Works

The platform follows a simple **search → discover → evaluate → connect → buy** journey.

### 1. Browse Available Vehicles

Visitors can explore vehicles listed on the Varban Auto Flex marketplace.

Vehicle listings provide important information to help potential buyers understand what is being offered before contacting the seller.

Depending on the listing, users can view information such as:

* Vehicle make and model
* Manufacturing year
* Price
* Mileage
* Fuel type
* Transmission
* Vehicle condition
* Location
* Vehicle images
* Seller information
* Additional vehicle specifications

This allows buyers to compare available vehicles before making a decision.

---

## 🔎 2. Search for the Right Vehicle

The marketplace allows buyers to quickly narrow down available vehicles based on their preferences.

Users can search and filter listings according to relevant vehicle characteristics such as:

* Make
* Model
* Price range
* Year
* Mileage
* Body type
* Fuel type
* Transmission
* Location
* Other available specifications

The search experience is designed to help users move from hundreds of potential vehicles to the listings that are most relevant to them.

---

## ⚡ 3. Real-Time Search & Filtering

Varban Auto Flex keeps the vehicle discovery experience responsive and efficient.

Search and filter selections are synchronized with the marketplace query state, allowing users to refine their results without losing track of the criteria they selected.

This makes it easier to:

1. Select vehicle requirements.
2. Review matching vehicles.
3. Adjust filters.
4. Compare new results.
5. Open individual vehicle listings.

---

## 🚘 4. View Vehicle Details

When a buyer finds an interesting vehicle, they can open the vehicle listing to examine its available information.

The vehicle detail experience is intended to give buyers a clearer understanding of the vehicle before they proceed to the seller.

A listing can contain:

* Vehicle photographs
* Vehicle specifications
* Asking price
* Location
* Seller details
* Description
* Availability information
* Other relevant listing information

The goal is to give buyers enough information to make an informed decision before initiating a purchase conversation.

---

## 👤 5. Sellers Can Submit Vehicles

Vehicle owners, dealers, and car-yard businesses can submit vehicles to the marketplace.

The vehicle submission process allows sellers to provide the information required to create a marketplace listing.

A typical submission includes:

* Vehicle details
* Pricing information
* Vehicle specifications
* Description
* Vehicle photographs
* Seller information
* Supporting documentation where required

Submitted information is handled through the platform's backend and database security architecture.

---

## 📄 6. Vehicle Documentation & Logbook Protection

Varban Auto Flex is designed with additional protection around sensitive vehicle documentation.

Where seller documentation is required, supporting documents such as logbook-related information are handled separately from publicly visible vehicle information.

This helps protect sensitive seller documentation while allowing the marketplace to display only the information necessary for vehicle discovery.

---

## 💳 7. Payments & Transaction Support

Varban Auto Flex is designed to support Kenyan payment workflows.

The platform architecture supports configurable payment providers, including:

* **Safaricom Daraja / M-Pesa**
* **PayHero**

This allows the platform to accommodate different payment and transaction requirements as the marketplace grows.

Payment processing is designed to keep sensitive payment credentials on the server side rather than exposing them to visitors through the browser.

Users can access the marketplace from:

* Desktop computers

The interface adapts to different screen sizes so that users can browse and interact with vehicle listings conveniently whether they are at home, at a car yard, or on the move.

## 🔐 Security & Data Protection

Varban Auto Flex is built with security-conscious architecture for handling marketplace information.

The platform uses:

* Supabase database services
* Row Level Security (RLS)
* Protected seller documentation
* Server-side handling of sensitive credentials
* Secure API architecture
* Protected payment-provider credentials
* Controlled access to sensitive backend operations

Sensitive backend credentials such as payment secrets, service-role credentials, and email API keys are not intended to be exposed to website visitors.

---

## 📧 Communication & Notifications

The platform architecture supports transactional email communication through **Resend**.

This can be used for appropriate marketplace communications such as:

* Account-related notifications
* Vehicle submission notifications

* Seller/buyer updates
* System notifications
